Apple iOS Lock Screen Setup

  1. Export Wallpaper: Tap "Export 4K Wallpaper" and save the full-resolution PNG to your Photos.
  2. Lock Screen Gallery: Wake your iPhone and long-press anywhere on the Lock Screen until the wallpaper gallery opens.
  3. Add New: Tap the blue + button in the bottom right corner.
  4. Select Photos: Choose the exported AuraWall image from your recents.
  5. Depth Effect: Notice how iOS automatically activates the depth effect, tucking the clock numerals behind your subject's head/contour.
  6. Save Pair: Tap "Add" in the top right, then tap "Set as Wallpaper Pair".
